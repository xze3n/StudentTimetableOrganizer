# Student Timetable Organizer

Timetable organization for students at UBB Cluj's Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science.

## Language

**Official timetable**:
The faculty's published schedule of teaching activities for a particular academic year and semester.
_Avoid_: Course material, course documentation

**Personal timetable**:
The schedule of teaching activities selected for an individual student.
_Avoid_: Faculty timetable

**Timetable question**:
A question about scheduled teaching activities or organizing a personal timetable, rather than the subject matter taught in a course.
_Avoid_: Course-content question

**Group alternative**:
A published seminar or lab session for the same activity in another group within the student's specialization, teaching language, and study year. Its inclusion in a proposed personal timetable does not establish permission to attend.
_Avoid_: Approved transfer

**Refused alternative**:
A specific seminar or lab group alternative that a student reports they cannot attend in the selected semester.
_Avoid_: Unavailable course

**Hard constraint**:
A requirement a proposed personal timetable must satisfy, such as a stated period when the student cannot attend.
_Avoid_: Preference

**Preference**:
A desired property of a personal timetable that may be traded off when finding a valid schedule.
_Avoid_: Hard constraint
